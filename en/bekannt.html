Knowledge base

What do we know so far?

The pre-release state of knowledge – after launch this section becomes the full guide (missions, map, vehicles, weapons, GTA Online).

Setting Official

The fictional state of Leonida – Rockstar's modern take on Florida – with Vice City (Miami) at its center. Set in the present day.

Protagonists Official

Jason Duval & Lucia Caminos – a playable duo for the first time, and with Lucia the first playable female lead of a mainline entry. Lucia hails from Liberty City; the pair's starting apartment sits in the Leonida Keys.

Single-player & Online Official

The campaign is playable offline. A GTA Online component is confirmed, details to follow. Rockstar has also announced continued expansion of the world after launch.

Map size Fan analysis

Fan reconstructions from trailers, screenshots and the 2022 leak estimate the map at nearly twice the size of GTA V's. No official numbers exist.

The regions of Leonida

Rockstar itself has presented these areas via screenshots and descriptions: Official

Vice City

The heart of the game: Miami flair between art deco hotels, beaches, Little Cuba and the cruise port. Confirmed districts include Vice Beach, Ocean Beach, Little Haiti, Little Havana and South Beach – plus airport and port.

Leonida Keys

A tropical island chain in the south modeled on the Florida Keys, including Key Lento. Jason and Lucia's story begins here.

Grassrivers

Rockstar's Everglades: swampland with mangroves and alligators in the map's south.

Port Gellhorn

A run-down coastal town of cheap motels and closed attractions – Leonida's "forgotten coast", inspired by Panama City.

Ambrosia

An inland industrial town shaped by its sugar refinery – and a local biker gang.

Mount Kalaga National Park

Forests, canyons and rivers in the north: hunting, fishing, off-road trails – inspired by northern Florida and Georgia.

Counties & further places Fan analysis

From road signs, license plates and trailer details the community has reconstructed at least five counties: Vice-Dale, Leonard, Ambrosia, Kelly and Mariana – plus Lake Leonida at the map's center. License plates reading "Gloriana" also hint at a second, Georgia-inspired state; whether it's accessible remains open.

From the 2022 leak Unconfirmed

Leaked development material suggests: over 700 enterable buildings and shops (clubs, motels, pawn shops, gun stores), a working tram stopping at the airport, explorable underwater areas and a wildlife system from alligators to bobcats. None of it officially confirmed – and much may have changed since 2022.
