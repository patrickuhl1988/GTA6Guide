Briefing

The game in 30 seconds

GTA VI is set in the present day in the state of Leonida (Florida) with Vice City at its heart. For the first time you control a duo: Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos – the first playable female protagonist in a mainline GTA. The campaign is playable offline; GTA Online is confirmed. Rockstar has already revealed six regions, from the Leonida Keys to Mount Kalaga National Park.

Quick answers

GTA 6: Frequently asked questions

When does GTA 6 release?

November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Preload starts November 12, 2026.

How much does GTA 6 cost?

Standard Edition $79.99 / €79.99, Ultimate Edition $99.99 / €99.99. Upgrading from Standard to Ultimate costs $20 later.

Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

No PC version is announced. Rockstar traditionally ships PC versions 1–2 years later, so late 2027 or early 2028 is expected. Beware of any "PC download" offers today.

Is there a disc version?

No – even the retail box contains only a download code. Ultimate is digital only.

Does GTA 6 have an online mode?

Yes, GTA Online is confirmed, details to follow. The story campaign is fully playable offline.
